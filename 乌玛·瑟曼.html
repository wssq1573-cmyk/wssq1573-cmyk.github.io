简介Introduction

UMA THURMAN

乌玛·瑟曼（Uma Thurman），1970年4月29日出生于美国马萨诸塞州波士顿，身高178cm，早年以模特身份出道。她的名字源自藏传佛教女神，意为“同情与怜悯”——父亲是哥伦比亚大学宗教学教授，母亲是瑞典籍心理分析医生。

Uma Thurman, born April 29, 1970, in Boston, Massachusetts, stands 178 cm tall and began her career as a model. Her name comes from a goddess in Tibetan Buddhism, meaning “compassion and mercy” — her father was a professor of religion at Columbia University, and her mother is a Swedish-born psychotherapist.

1994年，她因昆汀·塔伦蒂诺的《低俗小说》中“米娅”一角提名奥斯卡最佳女配角，片中迷离眼神的画面成为影史经典。2003至2004年，她再度与昆汀合作《杀死比尔》系列，饰演复仇的“新娘”，获金球奖剧情类最佳女主角提名，奠定动作女星地位。她以独特气质游走于商业与艺术之间，是昆汀·塔伦蒂诺的银幕缪斯。

In 1994, her role as “Mia” in Quentin Tarantino's Pulp Fiction earned her an Academy Award nomination for Best Supporting Actress, and the film's dreamlike gaze became a classic of cinema history. From 2003 to 2004, she reunited with Tarantino for the Kill Bill series, playing the avenging “Bride,” earning a Golden Globe nomination for Best Actress in a Drama and cementing her place as an action star. Moving between commercial and art cinema with her singular presence, she remains Tarantino's silver-screen muse.

“I'm not interested in being a movie star. I'm interested in being an actor.” —— 乌玛·瑟曼— Uma Thurman
